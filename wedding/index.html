Our story

A little bit of coincidence, or maybe fate.

Maral and Armin's story began almost ten years ago at a lounge during an open mic night. Maral's friend knew one of the performers, who happened to be a friend of Armin's, and the two ended up sitting in the same area and talking that night.

A few days later, they crossed paths again completely unexpectedly. They both had separate plans that day, but at the last minute each changed their mind and somehow ended up at the same café at the same time. Looking back, it feels like one of those moments that was simply meant to happen. From that day on, they were inseparable.

Over the years, Maral and Armin have grown together and built a life side by side. They've been there for each other through moves, graduations, career changes, moving in together, difficult moments, happy ones, and everything in between. Through every new chapter, they've continued to choose each other.

In June 2026, nearly a decade after they first met, they took their first resort vacation together. On a beautiful beach, Armin asked Maral to marry him, and of course, she said yes.

Now their story is bringing them back to the beach, this time surrounded by the people they love most, as they say "I do" and begin the next chapter of their lives together.

With love, Maral & Armin

Planning your trip

Frequently asked questions

We can't wait to celebrate with you in paradise! We've put together some answers to common questions to help you plan your trip. If there's anything else you'd like to know, please don't hesitate to reach out!

🌴Wedding & Travel Details

When and where is the wedding?

Our wedding celebrations will take place at Grand Palladium Select Costa Mujeres in Cancún, Mexico! Our group travel dates are November 22–27, 2027. The wedding day schedule and additional details will be shared soon!

Do I have to stay at the resort to attend the wedding?

To be part of our wedding group and receive our special group rates, guests must stay at the resort and book through our travel coordinator.

If you choose to book separately, we cannot guarantee the same pricing or group benefits. If you decide to stay at another resort, you will need to purchase a day pass to attend the wedding celebrations.

Can I arrive earlier or stay longer?

Absolutely! If you'd like to extend your vacation or adjust your travel dates, please reach out to us first so we can coordinate the arrangements and pricing with our travel coordinator.

Can I fly from a different city?

Of course! While our main group packages depart from Ottawa and Toronto, we can help arrange travel packages from other cities as well. Just let us know where you'll be flying from, and we'll work with our travel coordinator to get you the available options and pricing.

💌Booking & Payments

How do I book my trip?

To reserve your spot, complete the Guest Booking Form on our Booking page and email it to Maral at maralandarmin@gmail.com. She'll pass it on to our travel coordinator, Marsha.

A $300 CAD non-refundable deposit per person is required to secure your reservation.

When is the booking deadline?

The deadline to submit your deposit is December 28, 2026. However, our group rates and reserved spaces are limited and available on a first-come, first-served basis. We recommend booking as early as possible!

When is the final payment due?

The remaining balance is due September 17, 2027. Please refer to your booking information for the full payment details.

Can I share a room with someone if we're booking separately?

Yes! Rooms can accommodate up to four guests, depending on the room configuration. If you're submitting separate booking forms, simply select the appropriate occupancy and include the names of everyone you'll be sharing a room with in the Special Request section so our travel coordinator can match your reservations.

What is included in the vacation package?

Your package includes round-trip flights, airport transfers, and five nights at the all-inclusive resort, including meals, drinks, and access to the resort's included amenities.

Is travel insurance included?

Travel insurance is optional and is not included in the listed package prices. We highly recommend considering coverage for unexpected changes, cancellations, and medical emergencies. Our travel coordinator can provide more information.

✈️Flights & Travel

Will the flights be direct?

Our group flights will be with Air Transat, and flights from both Ottawa and Toronto are currently expected to be nonstop. Final flight schedules have not yet been released and are subject to change.

Do I need a passport to travel to Mexico?

Yes! All guests travelling internationally will need a valid passport. Depending on your citizenship, additional travel documents may be required. Our travel coordinator can help guide you through any additional travel requirements.

How will we get from the airport to the resort?

Airport transfers are included in the group vacation package. Transportation arrangements and instructions will be provided with your travel documents.

❤️Wedding Day & Dress Code

What is the dress code for the wedding?

Our wedding dress code is Island Formal – Dress to Impress! 🌺

Think elegant, sophisticated wedding attire with a tropical touch! We encourage beautiful colours, flowing fabrics, and formal looks suitable for a beachfront celebration.

Please visit our Dress Code page for inspiration and more details.

Will the ceremony and reception be outdoors?

Yes! We're planning a beautiful outdoor celebration surrounded by the tropical scenery of Costa Mujeres. Please keep the warm weather and outdoor setting in mind when choosing your attire.

Can I bring a plus one?

We can't wait to celebrate with our friends and family! If you're hoping to bring a plus one whom neither of us knows, please check with us before adding them to your booking.

Are children welcome?

Yes! Our wedding is family-friendly, and children are welcome. Please make sure to include any children travelling with you when arranging your reservation.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We would love for everyone to be present and enjoy the moment with us! We kindly ask that you keep phones and cameras away during the ceremony. There will be plenty of opportunities for photos throughout the celebration.

☀️Resort & Vacation

What is the weather like in Costa Mujeres in November?

November generally brings warm tropical weather, with daytime temperatures often in the high 20s °C. Evenings can be a little cooler, and occasional rain is possible.

Is there anything we should pack?

Along with your usual vacation essentials, we recommend sunscreen, swimwear, comfortable sandals, sunglasses, and your wedding attire! Don't forget your passport and travel documents.

Will there be other wedding events during the trip?

Yes! We're planning some fun group activities and opportunities for everyone to spend time together throughout the trip!

That being said, this is your vacation too! Aside from the wedding celebrations, you're welcome to join the group activities or spend your time relaxing, exploring, and enjoying the resort however you'd like. There's absolutely no pressure to participate in everything!
