Our story

A little bit of coincidence, or maybe fate.

Maral and Armin's story began almost ten years ago at a lounge during an open mic night. Maral's friend knew one of the performers, who happened to be a friend of Armin's, and the two ended up sitting in the same area and talking that night.

A few days later, they crossed paths again completely unexpectedly. They both had separate plans that day, but at the last minute each changed their mind and somehow ended up at the same café at the same time. Looking back, it feels like one of those moments that was simply meant to happen. From that day on, they were inseparable.

Over the years, Maral and Armin have grown together and built a life side by side. They've been there for each other through moves, graduations, career changes, moving in together, difficult moments, happy ones, and everything in between. Through every new chapter, they've continued to choose each other.

In June 2026, nearly a decade after they first met, they took their first resort vacation together. On a beautiful beach, Armin asked Maral to marry him, and of course, she said yes.

Now their story is bringing them back to the beach, this time surrounded by the people they love most, as they say "I do" and begin the next chapter of their lives together.

With love, Maral & Armin

Good to know

Questions & Answers

When is the wedding?

Thursday, November 25, 2027. The group stay runs five nights, arriving Monday, November 22 and leaving Saturday, November 27.

How do I book?

Send your RSVP on the Booking & RSVP page. Our travel agent will contact you to confirm your room and flights and take your deposit securely.

Why should I book through the travel agent?

Booking with the group gets you our group rate and keeps everyone together on the same dates, so we can plan events around you.

Can I arrive early or stay longer?

Ask the travel agent when they contact you. They can quote different dates or a different departure city.

What's included at the resort?

It's all-inclusive: meals, drinks, the à la carte restaurants, pools, beach and most activities. Spa treatments, the Chic Cabaret dinner show and some excursions cost extra.

Do I need a passport?

Yes. Bring a valid passport and check its expiry date before you book. Make sure the name on your booking matches your passport exactly.

What's the weather like in late November?

Warm and sunny, usually in the high 20s °C during the day and comfortable in the evenings.

Are children's rates available?

Yes. Child and teen rates are listed on the Booking & RSVP page, and the resort has clubs for babies, kids and teens.

Should I get travel insurance?

We recommend it. Your travel agent can help you pick a plan.
